Official Privacy Policy & Data Disclosures

Effective Date: March 30, 2026 • Compliant with Google Play Data Safety & GDPR/CCPA Standards

🔍

1. Zero-Knowledge Cryptographic Architecture

ADERA Password Manager (operated by ADERA Security Labs) is engineered around a foundational Zero-Knowledge Cryptographic Principle. When you initialize your master vault:

  • Your Master Password is derived using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 100,000 iterations and a cryptographically secure 256-bit random salt.
  • Vault payload records (passwords, payment cards, identity credentials, seed phrases, TOTP secrets) are sealed using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with fresh 96-bit nonces.
  • Cryptographic Data Encryption Keys (DEKs) are kept strictly in volatile device memory and zeroed upon auto-lock or app exit.
  • No Cloud Decryption: ADERA developers never possess, transmit, or retain your master passwords, salts, or encryption keys. Decryption is mathematically impossible without your device credentials.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Memory Isolation (Google AdMob SDK)

To provide free access to users worldwide, ADERA integrates the official Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK.

  • Data Processed by Google: Device identifiers (Google Advertising ID), general IP location, carrier info, and advertising performance metrics.
  • Strict Memory Sandboxing: The Google Mobile Ads SDK operates in strict isolation from ADERA's cryptographic vault storage. Network calls conducted by AdMob are technically unable to access, query, inspect, or exfiltrate plaintext or ciphertext vault records.
  • For full disclosures, review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Android Operating System Permissions Disclosures

ADERA declares only minimal, auditable Android OS permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required exclusively for AdMob SDK ad inventory retrieval. *(Vault cryptographic keys and secret records never utilize network sockets)*.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Enables checking connection status before ad queries.
  • android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC: Facilitates local biometric authentication via Android KeyStore.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides tactile feedback during copy and vault interaction.

5. User Data Rights & Absolute Local Erasure (GDPR & CCPA)

Under global privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA):

  • Instant Local Vault Wipe: You have the absolute right to purge your vault at any time via Settings ➔ Security ➔ Wipe Local Vault. This deletes all database entries, salts, and hardware keys irreversibly.
  • Portability: You can export full encrypted backups (.adere format) or printable PDF emergency sheets under Settings ➔ Backup & Export.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: On Android, navigate to Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads ➔ Delete Advertising ID to reset or opt out of personalized tracking.

6. Enterprise Security Safeguards

  • Screenshot Prevention: All sensitive vault screens enforce FLAG_SECURE to prevent screen recording, screen capture, and recent-app previews.
  • Clipboard Auto-Clear: Copied secrets are marked with Android 13+ sensitivity flags and automatically scrubbed from the OS clipboard after 30 seconds.
  • Cleartext Network Blocking: Plaintext HTTP traffic is blocked app-wide (usesCleartextTraffic="false").
