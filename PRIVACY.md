# Adere — Privacy Policy & Commitments

**Last Updated: 2026**

### Local Vault Data & Advertising
1. **Vault data stays on-device**: Passwords, emails, addresses, recovery phrases, and account identifiers are stored and encrypted locally. Adere does not send vault contents to the advertising SDK.
2. **Advertising network access**: Adere requests a banner ad on the Secure Vault screen using Google's Mobile Ads SDK. Ad requests require internet access and may transmit device, network, advertising, and diagnostic data to Google as described in Google's privacy disclosures.
3. **Test ads in this build**: This build uses Google's published sample app and banner ad unit IDs. They display test ads only and do not generate revenue. Replace them with IDs registered to the published app before serving live ads.
4. **No account required**: Vault setup and use do not require an Adere account.
5. **Local cryptography**: Vault cryptographic key derivation, authentication, and encryption happen on-device using Android platform hardware and Java Cryptography Architecture.
6. **No AI interception**: Vault contents, recovery phrases, and passwords are never submitted to AI models or third-party APIs.
