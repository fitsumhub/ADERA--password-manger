1. Privacy Architecture & Principles

ADERA Security Labs operates the ADERA Password Manager mobile application. ADERA is designed with a strict Zero-Knowledge Architecture. Your passwords, credit card credentials, bank accounts, Wi-Fi keys, secure notes, TOTP 2FA tokens, and cryptocurrency recovery seed phrases are encrypted locally on your Android device and are never transmitted to, stored on, or accessible by our servers or any third parties.

Security Summary

🔒 AES-256-GCM

All vault data is encrypted locally using hardware-backed cryptography.

🛑 No Accounts

No user registration, email, or account creation is required to use ADERA.

👆 Biometric Unlock

Fingerprint & Face ID run 100% inside Android Keystore TEE hardware.

2. Data Handled Locally on Your Device

  • Encrypted Vault Contents: All credentials you enter into ADERA are encrypted using 256-bit AES-GCM with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 key derivation. Plaintext vault contents never leave device memory.
  • Biometric Data: Biometric authentication uses Android's native BiometricPrompt API. Raw biometric data stays securely inside your phone's hardware element.

3. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To keep ADERA free, the application integrates the Google AdMob SDK to display advertisements.

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: AdMob may collect Google Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, device model, and OS version for ad delivery, analytics, and anti-fraud purposes.
  • No Vault Access: Google AdMob has zero access to your encrypted or decrypted vault data.

For more details, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Instant Vault Wipe: You can purge all local vault data anytime inside ADERA under Settings > Security > Wipe Local Vault.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling ADERA immediately erases all encrypted database files from your device.
