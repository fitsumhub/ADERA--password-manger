1. Zero-Knowledge Cryptographic Architecture

When you create a Master Password in ADERA:

  • Your Master Password is processed through a PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 key derivation function executing 100,000 iterations with a unique 128-bit cryptographic salt.
  • The derived key encrypts your local database using AES-256-GCM, guaranteeing confidentiality and authenticated tamper prevention.
  • Additional secret keys are generated and sealed within the Android Keystore System, backed by hardware Secure Elements (StrongBox / TEE) where supported.

Unrecoverable Design Commitment

Because we do not store or transmit your Master Password or local database salt, ADERA developers cannot access, read, restore, or decrypt your vault contents under any circumstances.

3. Third-Party Services & Google AdMob SDK Integration

To support free application access, ADERA integrates the official Google AdMob SDK (operated by Google LLC).

Data Processed by Google AdMob

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, mobile operator, hardware model, and OS version.
  • Ad Metrics: Ad impressions, click performance, diagnostic logs, and fraud prevention signals.

Strict Vault Isolation Guarantee

The Google AdMob SDK operates in complete isolation from ADERA's cryptographic vault layer. Network calls made by AdMob cannot read, intercept, inspect, or transmit any portion of your unencrypted or encrypted vault records.

For more details: Review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps.

4. Android Operating System Permissions Disclosures

ADERA requests only essential Android permissions required to provide vault functionality and display ads:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required exclusively by Google AdMob SDK for ad serving. *(Vault secrets never touch network calls)*.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Enables AdMob to verify network availability.
  • android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: Enables local hardware biometric unlock via BiometricPrompt.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Delivers tactile haptic feedback when copying passwords.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used optionally for security warnings and auto-lock reminders.

5. User Data Rights & Absolute Data Erasure (GDPR & CCPA)

In accordance with global data protection standards (including GDPR and CCPA/CPRA):

  • Instant Local Vault Wipe: You can purge all local vault entries, cryptographic keys, and settings anytime in ADERA under Settings ➔ Security ➔ Wipe Local Vault.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling ADERA permanently deletes all encrypted database files and Keystore keys from your hardware.
  • Data Portability: You can export your encrypted vault contents anytime under Settings ➔ Backup & Export.
  • Ad Tracking Opt-Out: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads anytime on Android via Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads ➔ Delete Advertising ID.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

ADERA is a general-audience security application and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children.

7. Enterprise Security Controls & Anti-Tamper Safeguards

  • Screen Capture Prevention: Sensitive screens enforce WindowManager.LayoutParams.FLAG_SECURE to block screenshots, screen recordings, and app switcher leaks.
  • Clipboard Auto-Sanitization: Copied passwords are automatically purged from the system clipboard after 30 seconds.
  • Cleartext Network Disabling: Cleartext HTTP communications are explicitly disabled application-wide (usesCleartextTraffic="false").
  • Volatile Memory Scrubbing: Decrypted password buffers in memory are cleared immediately following UI rendering.
